1. Who we are

Lapa Studio ("Lapa Studio", "we", "us" or "our") is an independent mobile app studio based in Ukraine. We make apps for iOS and Android and publish them on the Apple App Store and Google Play. Lapa Studio is the controller of the personal data described in this policy.

You can reach us at lapa.studio.app@gmail.com.

2. Scope

This policy applies to every app published by Lapa Studio on the Apple App Store and Google Play (together, the "Apps"), to the services behind them such as accounts and sync, and to this website. Our current apps are listed on our home page. If an App shows you a notice that is more specific than this policy, for example when it asks for a permission, that notice applies to that feature.

3. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account information. When you create an account: your name, email address, the sign-in method you use (for example Sign in with Apple or Google) and an account identifier. If you use Sign in with Apple and choose to hide your email, we receive only Apple's relay address.
  • Profile information. Details you choose to add to tailor the App, such as your gender. Optional details can be left out.
  • Your content. What you create in the App: events, tasks, notes, checklists, reminders, tags, links, photos and their settings. When sync is on (in some Apps it is part of a subscription), your content is stored on our servers as well as on your device, so it can sync between your devices and be restored. Otherwise it stays on your device.
  • Shared entries. When you share an entry, the App puts it into a link that you send yourself, for example in a messenger or an email. The entry travels inside the link and does not pass through our servers. Anyone who has the link can see the entry and save a copy of their own, so send it only to the people you mean to.
  • Messages to us. Your email address and what you write when you contact us for support or feedback, including attachments.

Information from your device, with your permission

  • Calendars and reminders. If you connect your device's calendars or, on iOS, Apple Reminders, the App reads the events and reminders of the calendars you choose and shows them next to your own entries. The App does not create, change or delete anything in your device's calendars.
  • Photos and camera. You can add a photo from your library through the system picker, which gives the App only the photo you pick, or take one with the camera. The App stores a copy of the photo without metadata such as location.
  • Notifications. To remind you on time, the App uses notifications. For notifications sent from our servers, your device gives us a push token.

You can refuse or withdraw any of these permissions in your device's settings. The rest of the App keeps working.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage information. How the App is used: which screens and features are opened, actions such as creating or completing an entry, and session length. It describes how the App is used, not what you wrote.
  • Diagnostics. Crash reports and performance data: what happened when the App failed, and how long it took to start or load.
  • Device information. Device model, operating system and version, App version, language, region, time zone, and identifiers created by the App or by our service providers to tell installations apart.

Information from others

  • Sign-in providers. Apple or Google confirm your identity and give us your name and email address as you allowed them to.
  • App stores. Apple and Google tell us about your purchases and subscriptions: what you bought, when, and whether a subscription is active. We never receive your payment card details.
  • Websites you link to. To show a link's icon, the App requests it from that website. The website sees your IP address, as with any visit.

4. Health information

Some features may let you log information about your health, for example your menstrual cycle. This information is sensitive, so we handle it with extra care:

  • we process it only with your explicit consent, asked for before you first use such a feature, and you can withdraw that consent at any time;
  • we use it only to provide that feature to you;
  • we never use it for advertising or marketing, never sell it, and never share it with data brokers;
  • we do not include it in analytics, and we do not use it to train AI models;
  • you can delete it at any time, on its own or with your account.

5. How we use information

  • To provide the Apps: create and run your account, store and sync your content, put an entry into a link when you share it, show your calendars, and send your reminders.
  • To process purchases and subscriptions and give you access to paid features.
  • To support you and answer your messages.
  • To understand how the Apps are used and to improve them, using usage information that is aggregated wherever possible.
  • To find and fix crashes and errors.
  • To keep the Apps and their users safe: prevent abuse and fraud, and secure our systems.
  • To send you service messages, such as security notices or changes to these terms. We send marketing messages only if you agreed to receive them, and you can unsubscribe in each one.
  • To meet our legal obligations and enforce our terms.

We do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing that have legal or similarly significant effects.

7. How information is shared

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only as described here:

  • Service providers who process data on our behalf, under contracts that require them to protect it and use it only to provide their service to us. They are, by category:
    • cloud hosting and database providers, which store your account and content;
    • sign-in providers (Apple, Google);
    • push notification services (Apple Push Notification service, Firebase Cloud Messaging);
    • analytics providers;
    • crash reporting providers;
    • subscription management providers;
    • email delivery and customer support providers.
  • Apple and Google, which distribute the Apps and process your purchases under their own privacy policies.
  • People you share with. When you share an entry, you send its content yourself, in a link. Whoever opens the link sees what it contains, and a copy they save is theirs.
  • Your group's admin. If an App offers groups and you join one, its admin can see, add and change your events, which stay yours. The admin cannot see your other content, such as tasks, notes or reminders. When you leave the group or are removed from it, the admin loses this access.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it, only as far as it requires, or to protect the rights, property or safety of our users or others.
  • A successor, if Lapa Studio is merged, acquired or reorganised. We will tell you before your data becomes subject to a different privacy policy.
  • Aggregated or anonymised information, which no longer identifies you, may be shared or published, for example as statistics.

8. Analytics, ads and tracking

We use analytics and crash reporting to improve the Apps. Where the law requires it, we ask for your consent before analytics start, and you can turn analytics off in the App's settings at any time.

We do not track you across apps and websites owned by other companies. If we ever want to, or if an App ever shows ads, we will ask your permission first where the law or the platform requires it (on iOS, through App Tracking Transparency), and we will update this policy before that happens.

We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.

9. International transfers

Our service providers may store and process data in the European Economic Area, the United States or other countries. Where data protected by EU or UK law is transferred to a country without an adequacy decision, we rely on safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

11. Deleting your account and data

You can delete any entry at any time. To delete your account and everything stored with it, use the delete account option in the App's settings, or follow the steps on our Data deletion page, which also works without the App. We complete the deletion within 30 days.

Copies of entries you shared stay with the people who saved them: they are theirs, and we cannot reach them. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription: cancel it in your App Store or Google Play account.

12. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and get a copy of it;
  • have it corrected or deleted;
  • receive it in a portable format;
  • restrict or object to its processing, including processing based on our legitimate interests;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting what was done before;
  • complain to a data protection authority, in particular the one where you live or work.

Many of these you can do yourself in the App: edit or delete your content, turn off analytics, withdraw permissions, delete your account. For anything else, email us. We answer within 30 days.

California and other US states. You have the right to know what personal information we collect, use and disclose, to have it corrected or deleted, and not to be treated differently for using these rights. We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. We use sensitive personal information, such as health information, only to provide the feature you asked for.

13. Security

Data travels between the App and our servers encrypted (TLS) and is encrypted at rest by our hosting providers. Access to your data is limited by your account: rules on our servers let each person read only their own content, and the admin of a group they belong to only their events. Inside Lapa Studio, access is limited to what is needed to run and support the service. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

14. Children

The Apps are not directed to children under 13, or under the minimum age in your country, up to 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has given us personal data, email us and we will delete it.

15. This website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts, and its fonts are served from the site itself. If you switch it between light and dark, your browser remembers that choice in its local storage; it stays on your device and is never sent to us. The company that hosts it may keep standard server logs, such as IP address, page and time, for security and for a short period.

16. Changes to this policy

We will publish any change here and update the date at the top. If a change is significant, for example a new kind of data or a new use of it, we will tell you in the App before it takes effect, and ask for your consent where the law requires it.
