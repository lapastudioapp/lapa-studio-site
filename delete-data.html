Data deletion

Last updated: November 20, 2026

In short

Delete your account from the App's settings, or email us if you no longer have the App. We delete your account and its data within 30 days and confirm by email.

1. Delete your account in the App

  1. Open the App and sign in.
  2. Go to the App's settings, then Account.
  3. Choose Delete account and confirm.

Your account is closed at once, and its data is deleted from our servers within 30 days.

2. Ask us by email

If you cannot use the App, write to lapa.studio.app@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request". Please include:

  • the App the account belongs to;
  • the email address of the account (if you used Sign in with Apple with a hidden email, tell us so);
  • whether you want the whole account deleted, or only some data, such as your health information or your usage data.

You do not need to install the App to ask. We may ask one question to confirm the account is yours, then complete the deletion within 30 days and confirm by email.

3. What is deleted

  • Your account and profile.
  • Your content stored on our servers: entries, checklists, tags, links, photos and settings, and any health information.
  • Entries you shared with others: they are removed for them too.
  • Usage and diagnostic data linked to your account.

Data stored only on your device is removed when you delete the App. On Android you can also clear it under Settings → Apps → the App's name → Storage → Clear storage.

4. What may be kept

  • Entries that other people own and shared with you stay with their owners.
  • Purchase records, as long as tax and consumer law requires.
  • A record of your deletion request, to show we handled it, for up to 24 months.
  • Aggregated or anonymised statistics, which no longer identify you.
  • Copies in our server backups, until they are overwritten within 90 days.

Deleting your account or the App does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play account settings.

5. Questions

See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use, or email lapa.studio.app@gmail.com.
